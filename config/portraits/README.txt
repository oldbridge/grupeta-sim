Put rider portraits here (png, jpg, webp or gif) and reference them from config/players.js:

    "portrait": "config/portraits/xabi.jpg"

A square-ish photo with the face in the upper half works best (it is cropped to a square from
the top).  With "portraitStyle": "pixel" photos are pixelated to 32x32 to match the game's art;
with "photo" they are shown as they are.  Riders without a portrait get a pixel-art face in
their jersey colours.
